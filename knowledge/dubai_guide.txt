DUBAI TRAVEL GUIDE

Dubai is a major city in the United Arab Emirates known for modern architecture, shopping, beaches, entertainment, and luxury experiences.

MAJOR ATTRACTIONS

The Burj Khalifa is one of Dubai's most famous landmarks and provides panoramic views of the city.

The Dubai Mall is a large shopping and entertainment destination located near the Burj Khalifa.

The Dubai Marina is known for its waterfront promenade, restaurants, and modern skyscrapers.

The Palm Jumeirah is a large artificial island known for hotels, beaches, and restaurants.

NEIGHBORHOODS AND AREAS

Al Fahidi Historical Neighborhood provides visitors with an opportunity to explore traditional architecture and learn about Dubai's history.

Dubai Marina is popular for walking, restaurants, and waterfront views.

Jumeirah is known for beaches, restaurants, and residential areas.

FOOD

Dubai offers a wide variety of Middle Eastern and international food.

Visitors can explore traditional Emirati dishes as well as restaurants serving Arabic, Indian, Asian, and European cuisine.

TRANSPORTATION

Dubai Metro is a convenient way to travel between many major areas of the city.

Taxis are widely available and can be useful for destinations that are not close to metro stations.

TIPS

Outdoor activities are more comfortable during cooler parts of the day.

Visitors should consider the weather when planning outdoor sightseeing.

Exploring historical areas can provide a different experience from Dubai's modern tourist attractions.
