PARIS TRAVEL GUIDE

Paris is the capital of France and is known for its museums,
architecture, food, cafes, and historic neighborhoods.

MAJOR ATTRACTIONS

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most famous landmarks in Paris.
Visitors can go to viewing platforms for panoramic views of the city.

The Louvre Museum contains a large collection of artworks and is
one of the most visited museums in the world.

The Musée d'Orsay is known for its collection of Impressionist
and Post-Impressionist paintings.

NEIGHBORHOODS

Montmartre is known for its artistic history, narrow streets,
cafes, and the Sacré-Cœur Basilica.

The Latin Quarter is known for historic streets, restaurants,
cafes, and its proximity to the Sorbonne.

Le Marais is known for historic architecture, shops, cafes,
galleries, and cultural attractions.

Canal Saint-Martin is a quieter area where visitors can walk
along the canal, explore cafes, and experience a less touristy
side of Paris.

FOOD

Paris is well known for French pastries, croissants, baguettes,
cheese, crepes, and traditional French restaurants.

Visitors interested in local food can explore neighborhood
markets and smaller cafes instead of staying only around major
tourist attractions.

TRANSPORTATION

The Paris Metro is one of the most convenient ways to travel
around the city. Visitors can also use buses and walk between
nearby neighborhoods.

TIPS

Major attractions can become very crowded during the middle
of the day. Visiting popular attractions early in the morning
can help avoid large crowds.

Travelers looking for quieter experiences can explore Canal
Saint-Martin, residential streets, local markets, and smaller
neighborhood cafes.
